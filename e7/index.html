Exercise 7

Annotated Homepage

Description

Using houseplant.com, take screenshots of the full height of the home page at 1440px wide (or as close as you can get to that dimension. Assemble those screenshots to show the homepage in Figma. Once assembled, annotate the homepage to note the various parts of the site and what they are doing.

Requirements

  • Screenshots of full homepage (header to footer)
  • 1440px wide (or as close as you can get)
  • Screenshots assembled to show full site in Figma
  • Parts of site annotated

Final Submission

  • Turned in on time
  • All requirements met
  • Prototype link submitted on Canvas
  • Linked on your class index
